Proposed member settings

Clubhouse terms and etiquette

Sandy Sports Club has not yet adopted these online terms or opened member accounts. The account workflow keeps age checks, guardian choices and optional sharing separate so the founding committee can review them before launch.

Choose how you take part

Members may read club dates, published newsletters and released minutes. Sharing a profile, photographs, recipes, messages or invitations is a separate choice. An officer checks the age group without storing identity documents or a full birth date in the online permission form.

Under 16. Young members may read club information. The proposed private profile and member sharing tools require an officer to confirm the guardian's authority and permission. Social sharing remains closed in this age group.

16 and over. After an officer confirms the age group, the member may choose to accept these terms and enable eligible sharing. They can withdraw that choice later. A private item stays within the audience selected for it.

Respect people and Country

  • Ask before posting someone's name, photo, location or story. For a child, ask the child and their parent or guardian.
  • Keep addresses, travel routines and children's whereabouts out of public posts. Share a home meal address only with confirmed guests.
  • Use club channels respectfully. Bullying, threats, racist abuse and unwanted contact have no place in the clubhouse.
  • Keep private invitations and friends-only material within their chosen audience. A copy already saved by another person may remain on their device.
  • Respect Quandamooka Country, local cultural knowledge and places others choose to keep private.

Your information and choices

The system records the age band and guardian permission checked by an officer, the current version of terms accepted, and an audit of permission changes. Birthday greetings are optional and use a private day and month choice. The member can review their settings and download their own contributions.

Member recipes are visible to eligible signed-in members and officers after the author shares them. A shared meal host controls invitations and sees guest replies. A guest link belongs to its intended recipient.

Ask for help

Speak privately with the Secretary, Assistant Secretary or another trusted officer to correct an age band, withdraw a photo, discuss guardian permission or report unwanted contact. A young member may bring a trusted adult.

Report serious online harm to eSafety. Read the Sandy constitution proposal.
