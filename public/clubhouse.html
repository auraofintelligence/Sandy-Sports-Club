Membership

Join Sandy Sports Club

Choose adult, cadet, junior, social, casual or lifetime membership. Young members need guardian permission. The committee reviews each application before the Treasurer confirms payment and issues a private account invitation.

  1. ApplyChoose your category and give the club a way to contact you.
  2. Committee reviewAn officer checks the application and any guardian permission.
  3. Payment and invitationThe Treasurer confirms the fee and payment before an account is issued.
  4. Member clubhouseChoose notice and birthday preferences, follow your pack and renewal, and read member news.

Fees have not been set for Sandy. The Treasurer will publish the applicable amounts before payment is arranged. This page does not take a payment.

Read the proposed constitution before applying.

Add meetings to my calendar

The file contains private committee dates. Choose your private calendar when importing it.

  1. Save the calendar file and find it in Downloads.
  2. Open it in Apple Calendar or Outlook and choose Add. In Google Calendar on a computer, choose Settings, then Import & export.
  3. Check the dates in your calendar. Download a fresh copy for later changes.
