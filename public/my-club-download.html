How do I open my download?
  1. Choose Prepare my download. Keep this page open while the club collects your own contributions.
  2. Choose Save my ZIP file. Look in Downloads, or in the Files app on your phone.
  3. On iPhone or iPad, tap the ZIP file. On Android, choose Extract. On a computer, double-click it or choose Extract all. Open Start here.html inside the extracted folder to browse your entries and pictures.

Photos also have their own folder. The spreadsheets open in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

If you plan to close your login, save your copy first. If your login has already been closed, speak privately with the Secretary about a copy of your remaining records.
