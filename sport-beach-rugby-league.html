THE GAME

About Beach rugby league

Beach rugby league brings league skills, running and passing onto sand. Sandy Sports would invite established rugby league clubs to run skills and showcase days alongside their home grounds.

JUNIORS AND YOUTH

Juniors and youth pathway

Sandy Sports is meant to be a safe, all ages club. This is a proposed pathway, not an official one.

Junior friendly format. Rugby league in Australia uses modified rules and a SafePlay code for each junior age group. On sand, the Sandy Sports version would be a club format, to be confirmed.

Source: NRL: Play Rugby League

Proposed age groups
Juniors 8 to 12 and teens 13 to 17, alongside open, masters and family sessions. Age groups are to be confirmed.
