THE GAME

About Ultimate and flying disc

Ultimate is a non-contact team game with a flying disc. Teams score by catching a pass in the end zone. It is self refereed, built on the Spirit of the Game, and the beach version is five a side.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Official format: WFDF beach ultimate. Summary only; the official rules are the source.

Team size
5 players per team on the field (a 4 a side version is also played).
Scoring
One point for each catch in the end zone you attack.
Match length
Shorter than the grass game: played to a points target or time cap set by the event.
Field
75 m by 25 m on sand, with 15 m end zones at each end (WFDF championship size; smaller fields can be approved).
Key rules
The thrower cannot run with the disc. An incomplete pass is a turnover. Players make their own calls and resolve them respectfully.

Source: WFDF Beach Ultimate rules and documents
