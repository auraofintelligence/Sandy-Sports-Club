The original Sandy Sports proposal text, code and brand are stewarded by Luke Nathan Hayes / Strange But True under the [Strange But True Public Source Licence](LICENCE.md) while the proposed club is being formed. This public source licence permits specified non-commercial use and reserves commercial rights. It is not an open-source licence. A future club may adopt different terms through its own governance.

Concept illustrations are generated scenes, not photographs of a formed club, its members or an approved design. The venue, sport, LED cinema and committee concept images added in November 2026 were generated for this site by Grok Bot; the remaining concept scenes were reused from the owner's Ballow Road Sand & Screen Hub and community-club-builder-sandy-sports, and Mozzie from the owner's Amity Point work. The multi-sport filmstrip logo and favicon are extracted from Luke Hayes's earlier Point Lookout Beach Sports Club and Outdoor Cinema proposal artwork. Third-party material retains its original rights. The site does not grant rights in sport rules, funders' content, film works or First Nations cultural material.

The 10-12 Ballow Road aerial and ground images originate from the public property listing and are credited on the Places page. The 360 sample is from Luke Hayes's June 2026 site walk. Property photography remains subject to its original rights.
