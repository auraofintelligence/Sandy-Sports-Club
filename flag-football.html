A BIG CROSS-CODE OPPORTUNITY

Flag football belongs in the main event.

The NFL's Australia and New Zealand office and APAC academy are on the Gold Coast. Australian and US governing bodies are developing flag football, which makes its Olympic debut in Los Angeles 2028. Straddie could add an island training and cultural exchange opportunity.

GROWTH NUMBERS

More than a passing trend

The NFL reports more than 20 million flag football players in over 100 countries. Its Australian school competitions reached more than 500 schools and close to 100,000 students by April 2026. These are the NFL's programme estimates, not Sandy Sports participation.
