THE GAME

About Beach football

Beach soccer is football played barefoot on sand. Small teams, a short pitch and constant shots on goal make it spectacular, with plenty of bicycle kicks and volleys.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Official format: FIFA and Beach Soccer Worldwide. Summary only; the official rules are the source.

Team size
5 players per team including the goalkeeper, with unlimited rolling substitutions.
Scoring
One goal is one point. A drawn game goes to 3 minutes of extra time, then penalties.
Match length
Three periods of 12 minutes, with a running clock that stops for set events.
Pitch
35 to 37 m long and 26 to 28 m wide, on soft sand.
Key rules
Played barefoot. No walls at free kicks; the player fouled usually takes the kick. The goalkeeper restarts play by hand.

Source: FIFA beach soccer rules and how to play
