DISTRIBUTED ISLAND FACILITIES

Three towns. Several possible homes.

Minjerribah’s beaches change with storms and cyclones. A connected inland or reserve network could keep sand sport available, with each town taking a different role. None of these sites is approved for this proposal.

Concept illustration; no facility or event is confirmed.

DUNWICH (GOOMPI)

Two waterfront sites, different strengths

10-12 Ballow Road slopes toward the water beside QUAMPI and features in the Quandamooka Festival proposal. Its incline could make outdoor cinema dramatic, while courts, access and seating need careful grading. The former silica sand barge-loading area at the end of Cunningham Road is a separate, flatter waterfront option with wind exposure to design for. Neither site is selected or approved for Sandy Sports.
