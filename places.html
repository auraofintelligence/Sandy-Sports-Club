DISTRIBUTED ISLAND FACILITIES

Three towns. Several possible homes.

Minjerribah’s beaches change with storms and cyclones. A connected inland or reserve network could keep sand sport available, with each town taking a different role. None of these sites is approved for this proposal.

Concept illustration; no facility or event is confirmed.

DUNWICH (GOOMPI)

Two distinct options, one town

10–12 Ballow Road and the former silica sand loading site at the end of Cunningham Road are separate possibilities. Neither is selected. Land access, ownership, environmental history, cultural heritage, utilities, traffic and neighbours need independent work.

BALLOW ROAD

Sand and screen hub candidate

An approximately 8,000 m² idea in earlier work. Test sport layouts, events, access and coexistence with nearby uses.

AMITY POINT (PULAN)

Neighbourhood sport and club life

Explore the sports and recreation reserve without displacing the existing cricket field or current users. Sand courts, a shared clubhouse and night programmes are concepts to test with the land manager and community.

RESERVE

Amity Point (Pulan) sports and recreation reserve

A town foothold for recurring sessions, juniors, club crossover and adaptable event equipment.

POINT LOOKOUT (MULUMBA)

A visitor-facing sports park

Explore the sports park opposite Point Lookout Bowls Club for court and event possibilities while respecting its existing uses. A beach sport venue there could connect residents, schools and visitors.

PARK

Point Lookout (Mulumba) sports park

Assess circulation, dimensions, nearby users, lighting, parking and a suitable shared facilities model.

RESILIENCE

A network matters when coastlines move

Permanent facilities can sit off the beach while portable sport and mobile screens reach seasonal and temporary locations. The three towns could specialise and still share equipment, bookings, coaches and events.
