THE BUILT VISION

A sporting precinct that changes shape.

Design for multiple sand sports, night play, major events and daily community use. Choose fixed and movable elements for each site, then connect three towns with shared assets and operating agreements.

Concept image only; not an approved design. No facility or event is confirmed.

SPORT CORE

The sand and the game

Courts, larger fields, accessible paths and spectator circulation should be planned together. The surface specification, drainage, shade, net and goal systems, maintenance equipment and safe run-off depend on the sport and site.

COURTS

Mixed volleyball campus

Multiple training and competition courts, adaptable for other net formats, with officials, seating and broadcast sightlines.

FIELDS

Larger sport zones

Separate configurable envelopes for beach cricket, football, flag and rugby variants rather than assuming a volleyball court fits all.

AFTER DARK

Lighting and power

Sports lighting, market and festival circuits, accessible routes and quiet-hour plans designed for neighbours and ecology.

→

CLUBHOUSE MODULES

Demountable, extendable and site-specific

Shipping-container-based modules could expand into reception, meeting rooms, storage, first aid, change and shower areas, film production support and food service. The final layout depends on site, building rules, water, wastewater, ventilation, heat and accessibility. Modules could move if the site needs change.

PEOPLE

Dressing rooms and showers

Inclusive change spaces, family options, toilets, shade, drinking water and first aid sized to the programme.

OPERATIONS

Equipment and maintenance

Lockable sport gear, screen panels, tools, batteries, cleaning, inventory and vehicle access.

→
COMMUNITY

Meeting and media room

A flexible room for committee work, coaching, media training, film editing and club gatherings.

→

SCREEN SYSTEM

Permanent presence, mobile reach

A permanent outdoor LED screen on engineered support could anchor a major venue. Modular panels could also make smaller screens for clubs, travel between towns and support a managed hire programme. Screen safety, wind engineering, rigging, brightness, sound, power, rights, maintenance and storage need their own plan.
