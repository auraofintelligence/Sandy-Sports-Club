THE SPORTING HEART

The sand is an arena, not one sport.

Mixed beach volleyball anchors a full catalogue. Each club can bring its own sport, coaches and people to a different surface, with space and schedule decisions made together.

GLOBAL SCALE

A worldwide sport with a visible Australian stage

FIVB reports 179 national federations represented in beach volleyball rankings. Its 2025 World Championships in Adelaide assembled 96 teams from 40 nations. At Paris 2024, beach volleyball drew nearly 450,000 spectators. These are sport and event figures, not a forecast for Straddie.

CROSSOVER MODEL

Existing clubs gain another surface

Rugby league, cricket, football, tennis, bowls, surfing and other island clubs already have histories and members. Sandy Sports can host joint events and complementary training without absorbing them. Agreements would spell out coaching, insurance, equipment, safeguarding, schedules and recognition.
