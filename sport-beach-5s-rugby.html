THE GAME

About Beach 5s rugby

Beach fives is World Rugby's sand version of rugby union: five a side, quick ten minute games and no set pieces, which makes it ideal for tournament days.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Official format: World Rugby Beach Fives. Summary only; the official rules are the source.

Team size
No more than 5 players on the field per team, with rolling substitutions.
Scoring
A try is scored by grounding the ball in the opponents' in-goal area.
Match length
No more than 10 minutes, played as two halves of up to 5 minutes, with a short interval.
Field
31 m long and 25 m wide on sand, with 3 m in-goal areas. Organisers may vary the size.
Key rules
No scrums, rucks, mauls or lineouts. A tackled player must pass within two seconds. A size 4 ball is used. Contact or touch versions for each age group are to be confirmed by the club.

Source: World Rugby Laws: Beach Fives Rugby

JUNIORS AND YOUTH

Juniors and youth pathway

Sandy Sports is meant to be a safe, all ages club. This is a proposed pathway, not an official one.

Junior friendly format. Rugby Australia's Get into Rugby is the entry programme for children turning 4 to 7, and Tri Tag Rugby is its official non-contact format. On sand, the Sandy Sports version would be a club format, to be confirmed.
