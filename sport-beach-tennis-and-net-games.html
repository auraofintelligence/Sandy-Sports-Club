THE GAME

About Beach tennis and net games

Beach tennis is played with solid paddles and a softer ball over a high net on sand. The ball is played out of the air and must not bounce, so rallies are quick and social. Pickleball style games could join it if the surface allows.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Official format: ITF beach tennis (other net games: club format, to be confirmed). Summary only; the official rules are the source.

Team size
Usually doubles (2 a side). Singles uses a narrower court.
Scoring
Tennis scoring with no advantage: at 40 all, one deciding point wins the game. The match format, such as one set or best of three, is announced before the event.
Match length
Usually 30 to 60 minutes.
Court
16 m by 8 m for doubles (4.5 m wide for singles). Net height 1.70 m for women's and mixed play and 1.80 m for men's.
Key rules
The ball must not touch the sand on your side. One serve only per point. Pickleball and other net games would need their own surface and equipment checks.

Source: ITF Rules of Beach Tennis 2026
