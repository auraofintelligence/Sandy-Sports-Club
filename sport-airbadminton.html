THE GAME

About AirBadminton

AirBadminton is the outdoor game of badminton, created by the Badminton World Federation. A heavier, wind resistant AirShuttle and a longer court make it work on sand, grass or hard ground.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Official format: BWF AirBadminton. Summary only; the official rules are the source.

Team size
Doubles (2 a side) or triples (3 a side). In triples, no player may hit two returns in a row.
Scoring
Rally point scoring. Current BWF guidance is best of five games to 9 points, with a two point lead needed from 8 all and a cap at 13.
Match length
Usually 20 to 30 minutes.
Court
16 m by 6 m, with a 2 m dead zone in front of the net on each side. Net height 1.45 m.
Key rules
The shuttle must clear the net and land beyond the dead zone on serve. Players change ends between games.

Source: BWF AirBadminton: how to play
