THE GAME

About Netball crossover

Netball crossover invites existing netball clubs to try their footwork, passing and shooting on sand, as a social game or a fresh training option, while their home courts stay their home.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Club format, to be confirmed. Details below are a starting point for the club to settle with players and partner clubs.

Team size
Club format, to be confirmed with the netball clubs. A smaller side may suit a sand court.
Scoring
Goals from inside the circle, as in netball.
Match length
Short social quarters or halves, to be confirmed.
Court
A sand court with two netball posts; size and markings to be confirmed in the space planner.
Key rules
World Netball rules are the reference. Any sand changes, such as to footwork or contact, are to be agreed with the clubs.

Source: World Netball: rules of netball

JUNIORS AND YOUTH

Juniors and youth pathway

Sandy Sports is meant to be a safe, all ages club. This is a proposed pathway, not an official one.

Junior friendly format. Woolworths NetSetGo is Netball Australia's programme for children aged 5 to 10, in three age based phases. On sand, the Sandy Sports version would be a club format, to be confirmed.

Source: Netball Australia: Woolworths NetSetGo

Proposed age groups
Juniors 8 to 12 and teens 13 to 17, alongside open, masters and family sessions. Age groups are to be confirmed.
1. Fun first
Come and try sessions and modified games that build skills and confidence.
2. Squad training
Regular training with qualified coaches for juniors and teens who want more.
3. Regional competition
Entering regional events through the sport's official competitions and partner clubs.
Brisbane 2032
About five and a half years away, the Games can be an aspiration for a teen training today. Selection runs through each sport's national body, not every sport here is on the Olympic programme, and the club does not claim a pathway to the Games.
