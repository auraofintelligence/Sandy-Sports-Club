THE GAME

About Beach handball

Beach handball is a fast, acrobatic throwing game on sand. Spectacular goals, such as spin shots and in-flight catches, score double, so the crowd is always on its feet.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Official format: IHF beach handball. Summary only; the official rules are the source.

Team size
4 players on court per team: three court players and a goalkeeper, with unlimited substitutions.
Scoring
A goal is 1 point. Creative or spectacular goals, in-flight goals, goals from a 6 metre throw and goals by the goalkeeper are 2 points.
Match length
Two periods of 10 minutes, each scored separately. A tied period goes to a golden goal; if each team wins a period, a shoot-out decides the match.
Court
27 m by 12 m on sand at least 40 cm deep, with a 3 m safety zone.
Key rules
Players may hold the ball for up to 3 seconds and take up to 3 steps with it. Only the goalkeeper may stand in the goal area.

Source: IHF Rules of the Game: Beach Handball (March 2026)
