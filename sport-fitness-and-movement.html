THE GAME

About Fitness and movement

Sand is a great training surface: it is gentle on joints and hard work for the legs. Fitness and movement sessions would cover warm ups, conditioning, mobility and recovery for every level.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Club format, to be confirmed. Details below are a starting point for the club to settle with players and partner clubs.

Group size
Small groups with a qualified coach or instructor, to be confirmed.
Format
Circuits, running technique, mobility and recovery sessions.
Session length
Usually 45 to 60 minutes.
Space
A marked sand area clear of courts in use.
Key rules
There is no single rulebook. Sessions would follow safe sport guidance, start with a health check, and be adapted for heat and ability.

JUNIORS AND YOUTH

Juniors and youth pathway

Sandy Sports is meant to be a safe, all ages club. This is a proposed pathway, not an official one.

Junior friendly format: club format, to be confirmed. The club would agree a modified junior format with coaches, players and families.

Proposed age groups
Juniors 8 to 12 and teens 13 to 17, alongside open, masters and family sessions. Age groups are to be confirmed.
