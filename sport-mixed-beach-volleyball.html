THE GAME

About Mixed beach volleyball

Beach volleyball is a two-a-side net game on sand. Teams have up to three touches to send the ball back over the net, and every rally wins a point. Sandy Sports would lead with mixed doubles: one woman and one man per side.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Official format: FIVB beach volleyball. Summary only; the official rules are the source.

Team size
2 players per team, no substitutes. Mixed doubles pairs one woman and one man; that pairing is a club format.
Scoring
Rally point scoring. Sets are played to 21 points with a two point lead; a deciding third set is played to 15.
Match length
Best of three sets, usually around 40 to 60 minutes.
Court
16 m by 8 m on sand at least 40 cm deep, with a free zone around it. Net height 2.43 m for men and 2.24 m for women; the mixed net height is a club decision.
Key rules
Three team touches per side, no player touches the ball twice in a row (except after a block), no open hand tips or dinks, and teams switch ends every 7 points (every 5 in the third set).

Source: FIVB Official Beach Volleyball Rules 2025 to 2028

JUNIORS AND YOUTH

Juniors and youth pathway

Sandy Sports is meant to be a safe, all ages club. This is a proposed pathway, not an official one.

Junior friendly format. Volleyball Australia's Spikezone programme introduces children (about 5 to 12) to volleyball and beach volleyball with smaller courts, lower nets and softer balls. On sand, the Sandy Sports version would be a club format, to be confirmed.

Source: Volleyball Australia: Spikezone and Have a Go
