SANDY SPORTS FILM CLUB

A second kind of team on the island.

The film club can programme outdoor cinema, festivals and a media hub while training local makers. It can film games, produce stories and create live event coverage with the sporting clubs, under the right permissions.

OUTDOOR LED CINEMA

Permanent spectacle, portable utility

The concept is a premium outdoor cinema: a large LED video wall, permanent or semi-permanent, set in a designed frame with quality seating for beachside or parkland evenings. It would need site approval, engineering, power, acoustic and lighting checks before anything is chosen. Mobile, segmentable panels could become smaller club screens and a managed hire fleet. The film club and asset custodian would coordinate rigging, transport, insurance, rights and maintenance.

CULTURAL LEADERSHIP

Make space for stories on their own terms

The separate Quandamooka Film Festival proposal and Minjerribah Screen Media Network provide connected ideas. Indigenous stories, names, imagery and community events require the relevant people to lead and approve their own work. No cultural endorsement is implied.
