HOW IT WORKS

Four ways in. Pick one or all four.

Join the club free, then open the Film club tab in My Membership and choose how you’d like to take part. You can change it any time.

Watch

Film nights by the water and at members’ places. Everyone votes on the movie and brings a plate.

Make

Shoot, edit and tell stories. Start with your phone and borrow kit from the shelf when you need more.

Sport crew

Film club games: 60-second highlights, scores on screen and, later, live streams of the big days.

Learn

One short skill session a week: steady shots, sound, interviews, editing, captions.

SUMMER SEASON · 8 WEEKS

From first shot to island premiere

Saturday 5 December to Friday 29 January. Each week has one make day at a club session and one short learn session, with film nights along the way. Each session is confirmed on What’s on once a time and place are set.

  1. Week 1 · 5 DecKick-off and kit check

    Meet the crew, list your kit and learn phone filming basics.

  2. Week 2 · 12 DecSteady shots and clean sound

    Holidays start. Tripods, gimbals, clip mics and where to stand.

  3. Week 3 · 19 DecSport highlights

    Film a social sand session and cut a 60-second highlight.

  4. Week 4 · 26 DecFilm night by the water

    Members vote on the movie. Bring a chair and a plate to share.

  5. Week 5 · 2 JanStories and interviews

    Ask good questions and film a player, coach or volunteer.

  6. Week 6 · 9 JanEdit week

    Cut your film in DaVinci Resolve (free) or a phone editor.

  7. Week 7 · 16 JanSound, music and titles

    Mix the sound, add titles and captions everyone can read.

  8. Week 8 · 23 JanIsland premiere

    Screen the season’s films and share them on the island screens.

Anyone can say “please don’t film me” and we respect it. Under 18s take part and appear on camera only with a parent or carer’s OK. Queensland state school holidays run 12 December to 26 January.

SHARE KIT

Borrow before you buy

Most of what a film needs is already sitting in someone’s cupboard. The kit shelf makes it easy to share without pressure.

List what you have

Phone, mic, tripod, lights, a laptop that edits. Your list is private unless you choose otherwise.

Lend it or bring it

Mark an item “lend to Film Club members” or “only when I come along”. Change your mind any time.

Ask, agree, return

Members ask for the days they need it. You say yes or not this time. It comes back charged and sand-free.

Club kit, same shelf

As the club gets its own gear through grants and the asset wish list, it joins the shelf with a kit steward looking after it.

Members only see your first name, the item and your care notes. Where kit is kept is never shown.

THE ISLAND NETWORK

Part of something bigger, from day one

The Film Club plugs into the island projects already being built, so a film made at a Saturday session can travel.

Minjerribah Screen Media Network

Where the club’s films and crew connect with other island film, media and training projects.

Straddie Noticeboard Network

Film nights go up on island screens and kiosks. Each night can be sent in the noticeboard’s own format for a steward to review.

Ready SETCO

Sustainable Employment and Training. Skills built in the season (camera, sound, editing, live crew) can feed into its proposed training pathways.

OPEN TOOLS

Your films, your files, your choice of tools

No lock-in

Keep everything in formats any tool can open: MP4 video, WAV sound, SRT captions and plain Markdown notes. Your work moves with you.

Free tools first

DaVinci Resolve’s free version on a computer, or a phone editor. Nothing to buy to finish a film.

AI as a helper, if you like

AI can help plan shots, draft captions or rough out a cut, and any tool can be swapped for another. A person always picks the final cut.

Planning builders

Turn ideas into film profiles, interview questions and run sheets with the Documentary Builders, and list shared gear with the Content Assets Kit.

OUTDOOR LED CINEMA

Permanent spectacle, portable utility

The concept is a premium outdoor cinema built from a mobile, segmented LED screen: modular panels that can be set up at venues across the island and joined into one large screen with quality seating for beachside or parkland evenings. Each venue would need site approval, power, acoustic and lighting checks before a screening. The same panels could split into smaller club screens and a managed hire fleet. The film club and asset custodian would coordinate rigging, transport, insurance, rights and maintenance.
