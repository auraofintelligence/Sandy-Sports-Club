THE GOVERNANCE INTENT
Safe, capable and inclusive leadership
Women-led decision making is a deliberate proposal, not a claim that officers have been elected. The builder defaults to women holding a majority of filled elected seats; it also offers a women and gender diverse equity target or a balanced recruitment policy. Every model keeps nominations open, shares practical work and requires a fair vacancy response. The founding group must review any binding composition rule against Queensland requirements.
