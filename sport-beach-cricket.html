THE GAME

About Beach cricket

Beach cricket is the relaxed, family friendly version of cricket played on sand with a soft ball. It suits festivals, club crossover days and mixed ages.

HOW IT'S PLAYED

How it's played

The essentials at a glance: team size, scoring, game length, the court or field, and the key rules.

Club format, to be confirmed. Details below are a starting point for the club to settle with players and partner clubs.

Team size
Club format, to be confirmed. Social games often use 6 to 8 players a side.
Scoring
Runs as in cricket; the club would choose any tip and run or boundary variations.
Match length
Short timed or limited over games, to be confirmed.
Pitch and field
A pitch strip with stumps at each end and a marked boundary. The outfield needs its own safety buffer from spectators and other courts.
Key rules
The Laws of Cricket are the reference. Beach variations, such as soft balls only and underarm options for juniors, are to be confirmed by the sport lead.

Source: MCC Laws of Cricket

JUNIORS AND YOUTH

Juniors and youth pathway

Sandy Sports is meant to be a safe, all ages club. This is a proposed pathway, not an official one.

Junior friendly format. Woolworths Cricket Blast is Cricket Australia's entry programme for children 10 and under, using plastic bats, softer balls and short modified games. On sand, the Sandy Sports version would be a club format, to be confirmed.

Source: Cricket Australia: Woolworths Cricket Blast
