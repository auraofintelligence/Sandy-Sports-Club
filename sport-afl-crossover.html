THE GAME

About AFL crossover

AFL crossover gives Australian football clubs a sand option for marking, kicking and conditioning, plus modified exhibition games, without moving the home game.

JUNIORS AND YOUTH

Juniors and youth pathway

Sandy Sports is meant to be a safe, all ages club. This is a proposed pathway, not an official one.

Junior friendly format. NAB AFL Auskick (ages 4 to 7) and AFL Superkick (ages 7 to 12) are the AFL's junior programmes, with junior and youth club football after that. On sand, the Sandy Sports version would be a club format, to be confirmed.

Source: AFL: Auskick and junior football

Proposed age groups
Juniors 8 to 12 and teens 13 to 17, alongside open, masters and family sessions. Age groups are to be confirmed.
1. Fun first
Come and try sessions and modified games that build skills and confidence.
2. Squad training
Regular training with qualified coaches for juniors and teens who want more.
3. Regional competition
Entering regional events through the sport's official competitions and partner clubs.
Brisbane 2032
About five and a half years away, the Games can be an aspiration for a teen training today. Selection runs through each sport's national body, not every sport here is on the Olympic programme, and the club does not claim a pathway to the Games.
